Chapter I: Happy Birthday! 🎉

Happy Birthday to one of my absolute favourite people! 🎂✨

Thank you for listening to my 100th "sorry." You make so many of my days lighter just by being in them.

Thank you for giving me so much of your time, for always being there to listen, and for being the incredible person you are.

Chapter II: Condition of Existence

“Of course you'll hurt me,
Of course I'll hurt you,
Of course we'll hurt each other.
But perhaps this is the very condition of existence. To become spring means accepting the risk of winter.
To become presence means accepting the risk of absence.”
And somewhere along the way, you became a very close friend of mine—someone whose absence I would always notice.

Chapter III: A Beautiful Trust

I know I'm not always a good friend, and maybe that's why I apologize so much. Perhaps I’ve just grown too comfortable with you.

Somewhere deep inside, I believe that even after all the arguments, there will still be an us afterwards. Maybe that's a beautiful kind of trust—though maybe it's also something I need to treasure and handle with more care.

Chapter IV: Rare Understanding

Many people know me, but very few truly understand me—and you are one of those rare few. That makes you my good luck charm, sis.

Dead Poets honor! Wherever life takes us, I will always be in your corner. You can confide in me without a second thought, just as I do with you.

Words have their limits, but that will never change how much I value you.

Chapter V: Promises & Wishlist

Promise me that every year, no matter where life leads us after school, you'll always find time to tie me a rakhi.

Our paths may diverge, but what we mean to each other never will. (And don't worry, I still have your wishlist written in my diary, and I'm going to fulfill every bit of it!)

Chapter VI: Missing You

We don't talk as much these days, and I miss you a little harder whenever I feel low and have no one to talk to.

I miss you a little harder every time I remember when we used to say good morning and good night, and when I wish I had someone who just gets me.

— Your Veera ❤️
